## Plan

- **F07:**
  - About: "No account and no cookies. Your scenario lives in the page's address, so anyone you share a link with sees its amount, currency, dates and settings. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits without cookies; it records which page, not the scenario in its address."
  - Error screens: "Nothing was saved."
  - Under Copy link: "The link includes this plan's amount, currency, dates and settings."
- **F08:**
  - Nav (top and tab bar) reads "Hedging (USD)", spoken as "Hedging, USD only". The Hedging lede's planner link and the planner's own links say the same where they point to Hedging.
  - On Hedging with another currency: a "Switch to USD" button that says exactly what it does, "Price USD 500,000 instead, the same number in US dollars". It never converts or rescales silently.
  - The planner links on Hedging then go back to the original plan (JPY 500,000). The original is kept in the address as `from=JPY.500000`.
- **F06:** above the chart, a group labelled "Start dates shown", and next to it the visible range: "30 Dec 2024 – 30 Dec 2025: 261 of 6,912 periods. Summary figures still use all periods." For All: "All 6,912 periods, 4 Jan 1999 – 30 Dec 2025." The note under the chart goes.
- **F09:** not current: "Not current: these are for your last valid entry: USD 15,000 by 1 Aug 2027, from 17 Oct 2026, 6 conversions, 0.5% fee, 2% target." Copy link reads "Copy link to that entry" and Excel "Download Excel for that entry" while it applies.
- **F05:** on phones, an "Explore past periods" link after the glance's links, to the timeline. It lands below the summary bar.
- **F03:** where a conversion's date had no published rate: "20 Jul 2026" with "scheduled 19 Jul 2026; the next published rate" under it, in the table and the phone blocks. The Excel's scheduled date for Target rate follows the same rule (the deadline if the target was never reached), and its column reads "Rate date (next published rate)". Labels only; the convention doesn't change.
- **Range chart:**
  - Compact axis ticks when the full ones don't fit (S$1.0T, S$1.5B, S$20K, with decimals enough to tell them apart). The full values stay in the chart's accessible text and in the table.
  - The median moves below the strategy name when the two don't fit on one line.
- **Comparison blocks:** a label stays whole; its value moves to its own line when they can't share one.
